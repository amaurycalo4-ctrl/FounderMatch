WHY FOUNDERMATCH?

You don't need another networking event.
You need the right person.

Students with business ideas often struggle to find people with complementary technical, financial, creative or commercial skills. FounderMatch is an experimental student project designed to test a simple question: can better matching help more student ideas become real projects?
